Old Monk can't call it rum

FSSAI says the Khopoli variants are a "rum-flavoured spirit".

Old Monk image from Neha
2Label claims FSSAI questioned: "7 Years Old Blended" and "Very Old Vatted".
₹350 crExcise revenue the maker says Maharashtra lost to the sales ban.

The drinks contain neutral alcohol and added flavouring. Mohan Rocky Springwater Breweries withdrew its Bombay High Court plea on 5 Nov and will submit revised labels, without the seven-year claim.

My take

Sixty-plus saal ki legacy, and the problem was one word on the label. Your brand story is only as strong as the claim you can prove, yaar.

The Dump04/16

The Dump

What happened, and what I think.

1

Diwali shopping was small and fast

85% of festive orders were under ₹1,000. Quick commerce volumes grew 75%, D2C sites grew 30%, and Tier-3 cities placed 41% of orders.

India isn't buying less, it's buying smaller. If your cheapest SKU costs ₹1,500, you missed Diwali.

2

Brands are building campaigns around Ramayana

Tribe Amrapali, Mangalmai, ITC Mangaldeep and UFO Moviez go beyond product placement. Dabur is holding back.

The test: are you adding to the conversation or just borrowing the film's fame? Most brands, sorry, are borrowing.

3

"Every Diwali Reel looks the same because the brief does"

Zoo Media's Pratik Gupta: 20–30% mega creators, the rest mid to nano, and sales metrics agreed before the creative.

"Content that ends in a basket beats content that ends in a like." Bas, frame it.

MediaNews4U, 6 Nov · afaqs!, 5 Nov
The Dump05/16

The Dump, cont'd

Brands playing a different game.

4

L'Oréal made a microdrama to sell "Ghosting Spray"

Urban Decay's six-episode "Ghost Hunter Hotline" was made in about four weeks and got 3.8M views in five days.

A product called Ghosting Spray in a show about ghosting. Itna on-brand hona chahiye.

5

Pinterest grew by doing the opposite of social media

CMO Claudine Cheever on reaching 640M monthly users by building around intent, not endless scrolling.

Everyone's fighting for your attention. Pinterest asked what you want to do next. Counter-positioning ka textbook case.

6

An Indian artist put Diwali on a Bayern jersey

Abhay Sehgal's hand-painted kit has elephants, a diya and a "00" for Aryabhata's zero. Kane, Neuer and Gnabry wore it.

No Bollywood face, just Indian craft. And that "00" detail? Screenshot-worthy.

Campaign of the Week06/16

Just For Hands

Wet Ones put "hand butts" all over New York.

Wet Ones image from Neha
Wet Ones · BBH USA · New York
What they did

Extreme close-ups of fingers posed to look like bottoms, on LinkNYC kiosks, bus wraps, a Times Square takeover and walls that handed out wipes.

Marketing Dive, 6 Nov
Campaign of the Week07/16
Banned.The MTA said it was too risqué for the subway. Wet Ones turned that into free PR.
The insight

Edgewell's research: more than half of people have used hand wipes on an intimate area.

The move

Say the embarrassing truth out loud, and be ready when someone objects.

How it works
1

Find the awkward truth

How people really use wipes.

2

Make it cheeky

Fingers posed like bums, all over NYC.

3

Ride the reaction

Goodwipes poked fun; the reply was up in Times Square within hours.

Why I picked it

Most brands hide the awkward truth. Wet Ones made it the brief. And when they got banned, they were ready. Shameless, in the best way.

AI & Marketing13/16

AI & Marketing

What the machines did to marketing this week.

1

Fresh Step made TV ads with AI talking cats

Clorox's cat litter brand went from brief to three 15-second spots in 30 days, at about a tenth of the usual cost. AI was used to test concepts before production.

Cheap is the headline, but the real story is testing the idea before making it. Strategists, ab bahaana khatam.

2

Reddit is testing search ads

AI chatbot traffic "has started to dry up": search referrals fell by more than 1 million a week in October. 72% of users come looking for information.

Everyone wants to keep your search inside their own app now. Where people talk about you matters as much as where you advertise.
