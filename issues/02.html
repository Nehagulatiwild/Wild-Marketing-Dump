The Dump04/16

The Dump

What happened, and what I think.

1

Reckitt is now TV's biggest spender

TV ad volumes fell 6% in Jan–Sep. Reckitt overtook HUL and owns 7 of the top 10 TV brands. Biscuit ads grew 74%.

TV isn't dying, it's just changing owners.

2

The world's top 100 brands: $3.9 trillion

48% of it is tech. NVIDIA grew 71.5%, Tesla fell 37.8%. Not one Indian brand made the list.

$3.9 trillion and zero Indian brands. That's the real number of the week.

3

Hyundai made a newspaper talk

A four-page Hindustan Times ad for the BAYON that plays audio, for a car with an AI voice assistant.

The car talks, so the ad talks. Medium is the demo. Love.

The Dump05/16

The Dump, cont'd

Big brands are rewriting the rules.

4

Clorox killed the annual campaign

Pine-Sol now posts 150+ pieces a month and keeps improving them after launch.

The one big annual campaign is dying. Brands now ship content like app updates.

5

Sephora became "Sethphora"

Seth Rogen plays a clueless gift shopper who calls pimple patches "face stickers".

Selling beauty to people who know nothing about beauty. Diwali gifting brands, take notes.

6

Audi's billboards give directions to donate blood

Signs in seven Danish cities: "Turn right in 250 metres and save up to three lives."

A car brand that gives directions. The purpose was hiding inside the product.

Campaign of the Week06/16

La Última Risa

Patrón put on a comedy show about death.

Patrón · New York
What they did

For Día de Muertos, Patrón staged a one-night, 75-minute multilingual stand-up show at The Stand in New York on 2 Nov, backed by paid social and posters across the city.

Marketing Dive, 27 Oct
Campaign of the Week07/16
One night.75 minutes of jokes about death, on the festival that celebrates the dead.
The insight

Día de Muertos already laughs at death. People write calaveritas literarias, satirical verses about it, every year.

The move

Go deep on one ritual of the festival, not the festival in general.

How it works
1

Pick a ritual

The satirical death verses.

2

Make it live

Turn the verses into a stand-up night.

3

Make noise

Paid social and posters across New York.

Why I picked it

Every brand does "festive vibes". Patrón picked one tiny ritual and made a whole night out of it. Diwali has a hundred rituals like that no brand owns yet.

AI & Marketing13/16

AI & Marketing

What the machines did to marketing this week.

1

Google's AI search earns less per search

Search ad growth slowed to 12% (from 15%), even as searches hit a five-year high. Google blamed "the lower monetization we were seeing in AI Mode".

People are asking AI everything. If nobody remembers your brand's name, AI won't either.

2

New CMOs are hired for AI skills

26% of CMOs hired this year have AI skills, against 9% of those four or more years in. CMO tenure is down 35% since 2010.

AI on your CV is the new MBA. Not optional anymore, bas.
