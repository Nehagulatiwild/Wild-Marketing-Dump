The Big One03/13

Ads now come with episodes

Godrej, Glow & Lovely and Maaza all dropped micro-dramas in one week.

2nd Gen Kumars · Godrej
Godrej

"2nd Gen Kumars": 8 two-minute episodes. The home is the hero, the products just live in it.

Glow & Lovely

"Saas, Bahu aur Magic" on ShareChat and Moj. A body swap, one variant per character.

Maaza

"Don't Go Na": a 7-episode romance at a Diwali party, by Ogilvy.

Not a fad yet: JK Super Cement ran 53 episodes. TikTok's "Screen Time" crossed ~580 million views.

My take

Honestly? Most of these are ads with episode numbers. Delete the logo. Would you still watch episode 2? If yes, you made a show.

The Dump04/13

The Dump

What happened, and what I think.

1

Myntra's ad bill is its biggest cost

₹2,326 crore in FY26: 41% of expenses, 1.9x its ad income. Revenue down 13.5%.

1.9x, wait what! That is not marketing, that's literally buying demand :)

2

D2C goes back to TV

CaratLane, Swiss Beauty and Dabur's digital brands are buying TV. CaratLane: awareness +54%, consideration +150%.

Everyone said TV is dead, but turns out Instagram reach is not enough if you want to capture your next million consumers.

3

Celebrity TV ads fell 17%

Jan–Sep, per TAM AdEx. Akshay Kumar alone is in 35 brands.

Akshay is in 35 brands. Name five. See? That's exactly why it's falling.

The Dump05/13

The Dump, cont'd

AI is doing the ads. Brands are doing less.

4

Meta's AI is selling the ads

Q3 ad revenue up 26% to $63.1 bn. Nearly 10 million advertisers used its AI creative tools.

Meta writes the ad, picks the audience, sets the bid. So what's left for you? The idea. Only the idea.

5

Coca-Cola: 400+ brands to ~170

It dropped "brand love" for a behavioural metric and moved North American media to Omnicom.

Even Coke is saying no to its own brands. And we're launching a new SKU every month.

6

Gemini learns your brand kit

Upload your website, fonts and colours, and it makes visuals in your style and remembers them.

On-brand is free now. Knowing what your brand actually stands for? Still not free.

Campaign of the Week06/13

Pubscription

Heineken made pubs bingeable.

Heineken · UK
What they did

With Amazon's Brand Innovation Lab, Heineken sorted UK pubs into "genres" by vibe, so you browse them like TV shows, even on Fire TV. Members get free weekly pints.

Marketing Week, 20 Oct
Campaign of the Week07/13
38%

of 2,000 people said they'd go out more if finding a pub were as easy as finding a new series.

The insight

People weren't short of pubs. They were tired of choosing one.

The move

Borrow the interface people already open every night, and package the choice like a show.

How it works
1

Pick a vibe

Pubs sorted into "genres", like shows.

2

Browse it

Scroll pubs like a streaming app, even on Fire TV.

3

Free pints

Members get a free pint every week until 6 Jan 2027.

Why I picked it

Because choosing a pub is harder than drinking in one. They didn't sell beer, they sold the decision. Total cheat code.

Heineken poll via Marketing Week

Why most cheese in our fridge is still imported in 2026?

Cheddar, Gouda, Parmesan… bro.

Category

Cheese. An aisle full of imports, and processed slices on everything: sandwich, Maggi, pizza.

White space

India makes more milk than anyone. Jammu, Bengal and the Himalayas already make cheese. Never heard of them? Exactly.

Wild Gyaan · Distinctive brand assets

The cues besides your name that make people think of you: a sound, a line, a colour, a character. Imperial Blue's new owner kept "Men Will Be Men" and the gazelle sound. Years to build, one rebrand to lose.

1

List it

What do people still remember? A tune, a line, a colour.

2

Freeze it

That part never gets "refreshed".

3

Hand it over

Give everything else to a new voice or format.
